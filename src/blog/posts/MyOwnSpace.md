My first personal website is up!

Though I haven't got the time to put all my ideas for my website to life yet. The functionality that suffices my main purpose - having a clean and aesthetic place whre I can store my notes and memories - is completed.GitHub Pages is a really convinent and friendly way for static website deployment.

Throughout my life, I've been liking the idea of journaling my thoughts. Even if I was just posting tweets on my protected account which had no followers. When I feel tired, I look back at my life and think that they are quite interesting to read.

What a fucking narcissist.

I then have been searching up others' personal website and blog since I wasn't satisfied of my layouts. I noticed that all the blogs I see, have several technical or academical posts. OK, maybe most people build a blog to share their knowledge, unlike me, is just someone who is tired of social media and want a place of my own. I either don't have the skills and knowledge to share, or interested in writing tutorials or documentations (which I think actually is a great way for self growth, but I simply don't have the energy and time for that, I am still busy learning how to walk). Seeing those websites made me feel inferior. I am not as good as them. I do understand the cause, I know exactly where I spend time on, wasted my time on. I still regret that every night. But after all I had my happy memories and it is good that I want to make changes.

Speaking of changes, with the help of my friend I met in my Master degree, I finally landed my first internship. Frankly, I don't think I am qualified eitehr, and the Founder is in doubt of my skills too. My friend still vouched for me. I am truely grateful and would like to give it a try.

This change pushed me to complete this blog section. It wasn't a lot of work, but I could procrasinate or even abandon this. I enjoyed the process too. I hope I will enjoy the internship as well.
